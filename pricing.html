WHAT IS A SCAN?

One scan = one evaluation run of one model.

Each scan evaluates a single model or endpoint against your baseline and thresholds.

YOU PROVIDE

A batch from production

Recent inputs and outputs from one live model, or a connected endpoint to sample from.

SENTINEL CHECKS

Whether it still holds up

Drift against baseline, recall decay, false-alarm rate, and output reliability against your thresholds.

YOU RECEIVE

A scored result and record

A severity-scored result, what changed since the last scan, and a timestamped evidence record.
