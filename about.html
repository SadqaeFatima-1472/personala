Syeda Beenish Fatima

FOUNDER, STRATAFORGE3 · AI/ML SCIENTIST & ENGINEER

Beenish works across explainable AI, multilingual NLP, agentic AI safety, and AI governance — building production systems for content auditing, model monitoring, and autonomous-agent safety across regulated industries including finance, energy, and industrial infrastructure.

She's the founder of two AI companies with live, pilot-stage products: MaqasidAI, a production content-auditing and compliance platform now in active pilots with industry partners, and Strataforge3, an AI observability and compliance-documentation platform for financial-services AI. Separately, she's designed and validated a constraint-and-audit architecture governing autonomous AI agents in critical infrastructure, stress-tested against real large-language-model agents across adversarial scenarios.

Before founding either company, she spent six years auditing production recommendation and advertising ML pipelines for Amazon and Google advertising partners across US, UK, and EU markets — hands-on experience with model drift, output quality, and multilingual data integrity at real production scale. She holds an MSc in Data Science from Superior University (thesis on multilingual NLP model compression for resource-constrained deployment) and is a published or in-review author with IEEE, Wiley, and Springer.
